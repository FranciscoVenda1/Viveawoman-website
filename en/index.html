Sofia, your intelligent companion

Sofia supports you in a personal, thoughtful way — helping you understand what you are experiencing and care for your wellbeing every day.

  • Understands your context — talk about your symptoms, goals and wellbeing routine.
  • Personalized guidance — wellbeing suggestions adapted to your moment and preferences.
  • Organizes your information — helps you record and make sense of what you are going through.
  • Prepares your appointments — supports you in gathering the right questions and information for conversations with healthcare professionals.
